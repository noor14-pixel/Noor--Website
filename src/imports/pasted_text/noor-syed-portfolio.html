Large-Scale RCT: EdTech Impact Across 560 Schools
CERP – EdTech Hub · University of Chicago Collaboration

Evaluated a tech-supported foundational learning programme across 560 schools and 80,000+ student observations in collaboration with Prof. Christina Brown. The key engineering challenge: a dataset merge rate of just 47% threatened to discard more than half the longitudinal data before any analysis could begin.

  • Engineered a probabilistic matching pipeline in Python that raised the merge rate from 47% to 95%
  • Applied Double Machine Learning (DRLearner / Random Forests) to estimate Average Treatment Effects while controlling for teacher fixed effects — isolating causal growth levers from observational noise
  • Conducted heterogeneity analysis by performance quartile, gender, and class level using Bonferroni-corrected regressions to produce a data-driven resource allocation hierarchy
  • Implemented K-Means clustering for student segmentation; translated profiles into targeted intervention recommendations for provincial government partners
  • Built interactive Power BI dashboards communicating causal findings to non-technical government stakeholders

02 Aug – Dec 2025
Testing the Homogeneity Presumption in Composite Household Indicators
Senior Thesis · LUMS Economics · Child Discipline & Early Learning, Punjab

A measurement study testing whether the practices pooled into a composite household-behavior indicator relate to the outcome alike, using the 11-item child discipline module of UNICEF MICS Punjab 2017–18 (15,590 children aged 35–60 months).

  • Proposed a joint, cluster-robust Wald test of coefficient equality; applied in an ordered logit for a 0–3 Learning Index (Cronbach's α = 0.81)
  • Homogeneity rejected for the eight violent items (χ² = 35.87, p<0.001), robust to weights, controls and each literacy/numeracy item, but not within psychological aggression (p = 0.897)
  • Monte Carlo simulations: near-nominal size, 63% power against a 0.2 gap between the two psychological items
  • Pooling conceals sign: explaining why a behavior was wrong is associated with a 3.9pp lower probability of no foundational skill, shouting with a 3.2pp higher one
  • Practices share no common antecedent profile (proportional profile rejected, p<0.001); exploratory Random Forests used only for ranking. Estimates are associational
Ordered Logit Wald Tests Measurement & Aggregation Monte Carlo Random Forest (exploratory) STATA Python UNICEF MICS
03 Aug – Dec 2025
Is Pakistan Realizing its Demographic Dividend?
Econometrics II · Time-Series Analysis · R

A time-series investigation into whether Pakistan's fertility decline has translated into meaningful economic growth through savings and capital formation channels — using 34 years of World Bank data (1990–2023). The answer: Pakistan's demographic dividend remains potential rather than realized, with weak capital formation as the binding constraint.

  • Identified a structural break in fertility data at 1990 via visual inspection and ADF diagnostics; restricted sample accordingly for reliable estimates
  • Applied VECM to model long-run equilibrium relationships between savings, capital formation, demographic structure, and GDP per capita
  • Bootstrapped Impulse Response Functions for robustness in small samples (n=34)
  • Built a mediation model to test the working-age population → capital formation → GDP growth channel; found the savings-investment link is broken in Pakistan
  • Forecasted demographic-economic dynamics through the country's demographic window (to 2045)

Can Audiences Tell the Difference? AI vs Human Screenwriting
Film & Media Studies · Quantitative Experiment · n=100

A blind within-subjects experiment asking whether audiences can distinguish AI-rewritten dialogue from professional screenwriters across five Hollywood films and five genres. The counterintuitive finding: participants rated AI dialogue higher on all six quality metrics (p<0.001, d=0.37–0.53) while correctly identifying it only 40% of the time — ten points below chance.

  • Designed a structured amplification-model prompt for ChatGPT-4 preserving scene intent while generating fresh dialogue; operationalised Bender's (2024) human-AI collaboration framework
  • Recruited 100 participants via convenience sampling (Facebook/Instagram/WhatsApp); administered blind paired-comparison survey across 5 genres
  • Applied mixed logistic regression to model correct attribution; critical attitudes toward AI, not confidence, emerged as the primary predictor
  • Synthesised Barthes, Danesi (floating interpretant), and cognitive Theory of Mind frameworks to explain why audiences project emotional authenticity onto AI output
